Side by side

Credit vs debit

Two live 3D cards, both rotatable. The credit card sits on the left, the debit card on the right. Click any marker on either card to read about that component. Physically the two are near-identical; the difference is where the money comes from and how the transaction is authorised.

Credit — left Debit — right
Credit cardDebit card

Neither type is better than the other. They suit different situations, and many people carry both. Terms, fees, protections and authorisation rules vary by issuer and by country, so your own cardholder agreement is the thing that actually applies to you.

Follow the money

How does a card payment work?

A tap that takes two seconds is a round trip across several organisations. Watch the pulse travel, or select any stage to read what happens there.

Handle with care

Card security

Different parts of a card carry different kinds of risk. Some details are printed on the card and seen by anyone who holds it. Others — your PIN, one-time codes, passwords — are authentication secrets and belong to you alone.

Never share your PIN, one-time codes, passwords or security code with anyone who contacts you — including someone claiming to be your bank, the police, or a delivery company. A genuine bank will not ask you for them. CardLab never asks you to type any real card detail, and nothing on this site is a form.

Quiz mode
